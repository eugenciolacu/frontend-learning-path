Features

Everything your team needs

From planning to delivery, Horizon covers the full lifecycle of your work without getting in the way.

  • Visual Boards

    Drag-and-drop Kanban boards that keep the whole team on the same page without endless status meetings.

  • Smart Timelines

    Gantt-style roadmaps that automatically adjust when tasks shift, so your plan always reflects reality.

  • Team Analytics

    Velocity charts, blockers overview, and delivery trends surfaced automatically — no manual reporting needed.

  • Integrations

    Connect Slack, GitHub, Figma, and Notion in minutes. Horizon speaks the tools your team already loves.

  • Goals & OKRs

    Link tasks directly to quarterly goals and watch progress update in real time as work gets done.

  • Automations

    Set up repeating tasks, auto-assignments, and status triggers without writing a single line of code.
